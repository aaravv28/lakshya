# 02: Recruiter registers and logs in

**What to build:** A new recruiter opens "New to Lakshya? Register as a recruiter" from the login page (shown when the Recruiter role is selected). They fill in two sections:
- **Your details:** name, email, phone, designation and password.
- **Your company:** name, industry, website, description, and an optional logo.

On success, the recruiter (Draft) and the company are created together, IDs are generated by the server, and the recruiter lands on the login page with "Registered. Log in to continue." They can log in straight away, before any approval.

A company name matching an existing one (ignoring capitals and spaces) gets "This company is already registered on Lakshya.", and a duplicate email is refused. Either way, the form marks the field and keeps what was typed.
